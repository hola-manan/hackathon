Service Architecture & Flow

A farmer sends a WhatsApp voice note, photo, or SMS in their own language. The platform recommends the right crop, tells them when to irrigate, diagnoses crop disease from a photo, and hands hard cases to the nearest Rythu Seva Kendra. Follow the arrows top‑to‑bottom for a request; coloured arrows show data reads, proactive alerts, and the human expert loop.

How to read it: One farmer message enters through WhatsApp (or the IVR/SMS fallback), is transcribed and translated by the Indic Language AI, and the Claude orchestrator routes it to one of three services. Services read soil, groundwater, weather, satellite and price data — ingested up from government sources into our stores and served through Vertex AI models + Earth Engine. Alerts push back to the farmer (terracotta); severe crop-disease cases loop to a human expert at the Rythu Seva Kendra (green) and the resolution returns to the farmer. The sensor feed is simulated + satellite today, with the same ingest seam left open for real LoRaWAN IoT.
